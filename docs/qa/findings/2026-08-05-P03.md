### Note — two doc defects found and fixed during the run

Neither is an app fault. Both were introduced when these two cases were
rewritten for the rulings, and both are the same mistake: a case that mutates
the shared `CHAR` and doesn't put it back.

- **P03-011** replaced the global `CHAR` with a fresh character carrying a
  deliberate 0-rank specialization, and left it there. P03-012 and P03-013 then
  ran against it, and P03-013 failed with a phantom
  `"Archery: a specialization needs at least 1 rank in the skill."` at the head
  of its error list. Rewritten to build its own character and call
  `RULES.calculate` directly, touching nothing global.
- **P03-004** also replaces `CHAR`, which it needs to (it exercises
  `seedClassicPriorities` against the live character). It now restores the
  previous one and recalculates before returning.

A case that leaves state behind doesn't fail — it makes the *next* case fail,
which is much harder to read. Worth checking for whenever a case is edited.
