### NEW-003 — FAIL
The Gear tab scrolls sideways at 375×812.

375px is **below this pass's stated range** — P13 starts at 834px and the app is
not designed for phones. Recorded here anyway because the stylesheet carries an
`@media(max-width:680px)` block written specifically to keep a phone usable, and
that block's own comment names the sideways-scroll bug it was meant to fix. Now
guarded by the new P13-011.

`documentElement.scrollWidth` is **389** against a `clientWidth` of **375** —
14px of horizontal page scroll, on the Gear tab only. The other nine play tabs
are clean.

The gear table itself is correctly contained: it sits in `.sh-tablewrap`
(`overflow-x: auto`). The blowout is one level up, in the narrow-viewport rule

    static/style.css:1647   .sh-two,.sh-pools,.sh-skillgrid{grid-template-columns:1fr}

A `1fr` track takes `auto` (i.e. min-content) as its automatic minimum, so the
card's min-content width sizes the column rather than the grid sizing the card.
Measured: `.sh-two` is 347px wide with a computed
`grid-template-columns: 374.547px` — a single column wider than the grid holding
it — and the child card lands at left 14, right 389.

Setting `grid-template-columns: minmax(0,1fr)` on the element in the page drops
`scrollWidth` from 389 to exactly 375. `.sh-pools` and `.sh-skillgrid` share the
declaration and the same latent weakness; neither trips today.
